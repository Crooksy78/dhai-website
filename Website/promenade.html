Questions You Might Have

Is it really free?

Yes. The Jam Session is normally $450, and it’s free for every business at The Promenade. No catch, and no obligation to do anything afterwards.

Why are you offering it for free?

I live just around the corner, and I’d like to get to know the businesses here. Some people want more help afterwards, and that’s how I find work. If you don’t, that’s completely fine.

When do I need to book?

Book before Christmas. The session itself can be any time in December or January, whenever suits you.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. You know your business, and that’s the part that matters. I’ll explain anything else in plain English.

Who should come?

Anyone from the business who might find it useful. The people who do the work day to day often have the best ideas.

What happens afterwards?

I email you a short list of the best ideas that came up. What you do with them is up to you. If you’d like more help, the next step is the AI Action Plan, but there’s no pressure.
