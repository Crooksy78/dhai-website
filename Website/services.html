Questions You Might Have

Why do I have to start with the assessment?

Because we can’t recommend the right fix until we understand how your business runs. The assessment shows us where your time goes and which opportunities are worth pursuing, so whatever we do next is built on facts, not guesses.

It’s also built to be worth the money on its own. Your report gives you a clear plan you can act on yourself, whether or not we work together. And if you don’t think it delivers what we promised, just ask for a refund.

Which option is right for me?

It depends on how hands-on you want to be. If you want to learn AI and run it yourself, AI Partner Program. If you want the work handled for you, AI Team Members. If you’re a larger business wanting both, Fractional AI Lead. We’ll recommend one in your walkthrough, once we know your business.

Still not sure? Start with the AI Partner Program. Once we’ve worked together for a while and you’re comfortable with how we work, we can move on to more custom builds when they make sense for your business.

Will AI replace my staff?

No. We use AI to fill the gaps and take repetitive work off your team’s plate, so the people you already have can focus on the work that needs them. The goal is for your business to get more done with the staff you have. As much as possible, we involve your staff in anything we build, so they have a say in how it works and can see it’s there to make their job easier, not to replace them.

How does the credit work?

If we decide to work together after the assessment, the full $1,200 comes off your first invoice, whichever option you choose.

Do I have to keep working with you after the assessment?

No. The report and walkthrough are yours to keep, and you’re free to take it from there yourself.
