The Contents

What actually goes on your page

A one-pager isn't a stripped-back website. It's everything that matters, in one place, in the right order.

  • Your business name and what you do — clear in the first three seconds
  • Your phone number at the top — tappable, so anyone on a mobile can get you without hunting for it
  • What you actually do — your services listed out, so someone scanning can spot the thing they came for
  • Who you work with, and where — the areas you cover or the sort of clients you take on, so nobody's left guessing whether you're for them
  • Who you are — a few lines about the business, and your face if you're happy to have it there. People like knowing who they're dealing with.
  • Why you're worth trusting — how long you've been going, qualifications, memberships, insurance. Whatever's true for you.
  • Photos of your work — if you've got them. For some businesses this is the whole pitch. For others it's not relevant at all.
  • Your Google reviews — if you've got good ones, they go on the page. You've already earned them.
  • When you're available — hours, and anything worth flagging like after-hours or weekend work
  • How to get in touch — phone, email, and a form if you want one

Most of that already exists on your Google listing. So "send me your details" is usually a smaller job than it sounds.
