Use AI To Find Quick Wins For More Enquiries & Sales

Two places an SEO pro looks first, and a free Skill that does it for you

Google gives you two free tools that show how your website is going: Search Console (how you show up in Google) and Google Analytics (what people do once they're on your site). They're full of numbers, and they don't tell you what to do about any of them.

Turning those numbers into a to-do list is the bit you'd normally pay a marketing pro for.

This is the kind of job AI is good at.

The free Skill in this post does that for you, in your own website data, and hands you a short list of fixes. Here are the two quick wins it looks for.

Quick Win #1: Pages Sitting On Page 2 Of Google

Anyone who does SEO for a living (helping websites show up higher in Google) knows the best place to start is page 2.

These are pages Google already thinks are a good match for a search. They're close, just not close enough. And very few people ever click through to page 2, so right now those pages bring in next to nothing.

They're the cheapest wins in SEO: the page already exists, and Google already likes it. It just needs a nudge.

To find them yourself, you'd go into Search Console and look for searches where your pages come up somewhere in results 11 to 20. You'd then prioritise the ones that are potentially the most valuable to your business.

The usual fixes:

  • A clearer page title and description that use the words people actually search
  • Answering the question more fully than the pages ranked above you
  • Linking to the page from your other pages, so Google sees it matters

Quick Win #2: Pages That Work On A Laptop But Not On A Phone

Most of your visitors are probably looking at your website on their phone.

A 2026 study of 6,500 websites by Contentsquare found 70% of visits came from phones. So if a page isn't working well on a phone, that's where you're most likely losing sales and enquiries.

The trick is to compare each page with itself: how well it converts on phones, against how well it converts on computers. When the phone version does much worse, something on that page is usually getting in the way on a small screen.

The causes are a short list:

  • Phone numbers you can't tap to call
  • Forms with too many fields to fill in on a phone
  • The button sitting below a big banner image, so people have to scroll to find it
  • A sticky bar or chat bubble covering the button
  • Buttons and links too small to tap
  • A pop-up that only shows on phones
  • Images so big the page is slow to load

To find these yourself, you'd compare each page's phone and computer results in Google Analytics, then open the worst ones on your phone and go looking.

What Does The Free Skill Do?

A Skill is a set of instructions you add to Claude, so it knows how to do a specific job the same way every time.

This one is called 'Low Hanging Fruit', and it does both jobs for you. It starts by asking for your website address and what counts as a win on your website: a sale, an enquiry, a phone call or a booking.

Then it walks you through how to download two reports from Google, one step at a time.

For Quick Win #1, it finds pages on your website that are sitting just outside of Google's first results page for searches that are valuable to your business. It then gives you suggestions on improvements you can make.

For Quick Win #2, it compares every page with itself, phone against computer, and flags the ones with a real gap. Then it gets you to do what most owners never do: open your worst pages on your phone and try to buy or enquire, like a customer would. What you notice, plus what it finds in the page code, becomes your list of fixes.

You get back a short report you can download, with the five to ten fixes worth doing first, how big each job is and who usually does it. The top fix from each check is written out for you, ready to use.

Because it's working from your own numbers, the list is about your pages, not general advice. AI is only as useful as what it knows about your business.
