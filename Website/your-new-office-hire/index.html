What Can The Agent Actually Do?

It’s more than a tool that reads invoices and copies information into Xero. It’s designed to take on much of the checking, coding and decision-making involved in day-to-day bookkeeping.

1

Collects Invoices From Your Email

The agent connects to your mailbox and collects new supplier invoices.

It identifies which emails contain invoices and which are quotes, delivery dockets, reminders or other correspondence.

The benefit: Less time opening emails, downloading attachments and manually sorting through documents.
2

Reads And Prepares Invoices For Xero

The agent extracts the supplier, invoice number, dates, amounts, GST and other relevant details.

It then checks this information against your Xero records and prepares the invoice with the appropriate account coding.

It can also handle more complicated invoices where different items need to be coded to different accounts.

The benefit: Much less manual data entry, even when an invoice isn’t straightforward.
3

Processes Multiple Invoices In One Go

For familiar suppliers with consistent coding, the agent can prepare a batch of invoices ready to be drafted in one click.

Your staff review the batch to confirm everything looks good, then the agent creates them all as draft bills in Xero, with the invoice documents attached.

The benefit: Instead of opening and entering every invoice individually, staff can deal with routine invoices together and spend their attention on the exceptions.
4

Recognises Potential Duplicates And Mistakes

The agent checks whether an invoice is already recorded in Xero before creating another bill.

It can also identify situations that warrant a closer look, such as:

  • An invoice that appears to have already been entered or paid
  • An unusually large bill from a regular supplier
  • A supplier invoice with different GST treatments across its line items

Rather than simply entering everything it receives, the agent flags potential problems for review.

The benefit: Fewer avoidable errors, less double-handling and greater confidence in what’s being entered.
5

Learns How Your Business Does Things

One of the most useful features is that the agent can learn your business’s bookkeeping preferences.

It starts with your existing Xero history to understand how suppliers and expenses have typically been handled.

Your staff can then teach it specific rules, using ordinary language. For example:

“Always put invoices from this supplier to Equipment Hire.”
“Put the drill under Tools, the gloves under Workwear and everything else under Materials.”

The agent can remember confirmed instructions and use them in future runs.

It also notices corrections made in Xero itself. If your staff change an account or tax type after approving a bill, the agent sees it on the next run and asks whether that’s a new rule or a one-off.

The benefit: Less time answering the same questions over and over. As it learns your suppliers and preferences, more invoices can be handled in batches.
6

Checks Supplier And GST Details

The agent can check supplier ABNs, recognise existing suppliers and identify GST treatments that need attention.

It can distinguish between invoices that include GST, those that don’t and invoices containing a mixture of both.

It also looks for the signs of a fake or altered invoice, and checks the ABN register before a new supplier is created in Xero.

If something doesn’t look right, it can bring the issue to your staff’s attention rather than making an unsupported assumption.

The benefit: Another layer of checking, with fewer simple mistakes making their way into your accounts.
7

Keeps Invoices And Emails Organised

Once invoices have been processed, the agent can file the emails into the appropriate folders, attach invoice documents to the corresponding draft bills in Xero, and save invoice copies according to your business’s filing conventions.

Items that couldn’t be completed are identified for follow-up.

The benefit: Less filing and searching for paperwork, and a clearer record of what has been processed.
8

Gives You A Clear Summary Of What’s Been Done

At the end of a run, the agent provides a summary showing which invoices have been drafted, what was already recorded, what needs attention and what still needs to be done in Xero.

It also checks the work completed during the run against the records in Xero and the decisions your staff made.

The benefit: Your staff can see what’s been completed, what’s outstanding and where they need to focus next.

Question: Isn’t This What Hubdoc Or Xero’s Own Tools Already Do?

Partly. Xero’s built-in tools read an invoice and fill in the supplier’s default account. That works well when every invoice from a supplier always goes to the same place.

This agent goes further.

It works out the coding from how your business has actually coded that supplier before, splits an invoice across accounts when it needs to, catches duplicates Xero doesn’t, and learns from every correction your staff make.

It also turns card payments with no invoice into proper supplier bills. Xero’s bank rules record those as Spend Money rather than bills, so they’re missing from your Aged Payables and supplier bill reports.

If every one of your suppliers always goes to the same account, Hubdoc may already be enough.

This agent earns its keep when the coding takes judgement.

Xero’s tools fill in fields. This agent makes the decision, shows its reasons and learns from your corrections.

It Can Also Help With Bank Reconciliation

Supplier invoices are only one part of the bookkeeping workload.

The agent can also help your staff work through bank transactions that haven’t yet been reconciled in Xero.

It uses your existing transaction history and bookkeeping rules to identify likely matches, suggest account codes and help deal with transactions that have nothing obvious to match against.

For regular expenses, it can group similar transactions so your staff don’t have to make the same decision repeatedly.

It also looks for transactions that might already be accounted for, helping avoid recording the same expense twice.

A card payment with no invoice behind it is drafted as a proper bill to that supplier, dated the day it left the bank, rather than disappearing into Spend Money. Your supplier totals in Xero reflect everything you’ve actually paid them, including the things that went on the card.

Your staff still have the final say.

They review the suggestions, authorise any records the agent needs to create, and complete the final reconciliation in Xero.

The goal isn’t to remove your staff from the process. It’s to remove much of the repetitive checking and decision-making so the work gets done faster.

And It Can Help You Uncover Bookkeeping Errors

Beyond processing new invoices and bank transactions, the agent includes a separate ‘Health Check’ tool.

It reviews existing Xero records and highlights things that may warrant a closer look, such as:

  • Possible duplicate bills or supplier contacts
  • GST amounts that don’t appear to add up
  • GST treatments that differ from the usual treatment for an account
  • GST claimed from suppliers who weren’t registered for GST at the relevant time

It gives your staff a list of findings to review, including why each item was flagged.

It’s designed to be run each quarter as part of preparing the BAS.

Your staff work through the findings and tidy up anything that needs it before the figures go to your bookkeeper or accountant, rather than having problems come back to you after lodgement.

This doesn’t replace your bookkeeper or accountant, and it doesn’t make corrections or lodge anything on your behalf.

How Does It Work In Your Business?

The agent is configured for the way your business already operates. It’s not a generic chatbot that needs to be taught your bookkeeping from scratch every time you use it.

  1. 1

    We Set It Up Around Your Xero File

    We start by reviewing your existing Xero setup, chart of accounts, suppliers and bookkeeping history.

    This helps establish how your business codes invoices, deals with regular suppliers and handles different types of transactions.

    We also connect the relevant email account and configure the agent around your existing processes.

  2. 2

    Your Staff Start A Run When They’re Ready

    The agent operates through a conversational desktop interface.

    Rather than learning complicated software or writing technical AI prompts, your staff can simply start a bookkeeping run.

    The agent collects the invoices that have arrived since the previous run, reads them and determines what can be prepared straight away and what needs a decision.

    You can run it as often as suits your business: weekly, more frequently, or whenever there’s a batch of invoices to process.

  3. 3

    The Agent Does The Preparation. Your Staff Make The Decisions.

    Routine invoices are grouped together for review.

    Where the agent needs clarification, it asks straightforward questions with numbered options to make responding quick & easy.

    Your staff can also type instructions in plain English.

    Once they’ve confirmed the proposed entries, the agent creates the draft bills in Xero.

  4. 4

    Your Staff Review And Approve In Xero

    By default the agent prepares draft bills, and your staff review and approve them using your normal Xero processes.

    As you become more comfortable, it can be set up to approve routine bills too. It never pays suppliers.

    The agent then provides a summary of what’s been completed and what still needs attention.

    As your staff correct entries and establish preferences, the agent can use those instructions to make future runs more efficient.

A Few Things Worth Knowing

It doesn’t replace your bookkeeper or accountant

The agent is designed to reduce the repetitive work involved in bookkeeping, not take responsibility for financial decisions, BAS preparation or professional accounting advice.

You remain in control of what goes into Xero

The agent requires human confirmation before creating records in Xero. Bills start as drafts, and you choose whether it ever approves routine ones. Payments always remain with your business.

It works with your existing Xero setup

You don’t need to move to a new accounting platform. The agent is configured to work with your existing Xero organisation and supported email system.

It does the preparation, not the approving

The agent sits on top of Xero’s own Bills and reconcile screens. Your staff still work in Xero the way they always have. The agent does the reading, checking and coding before they get there.

Every run leaves a record

Each run produces a plain-English summary of what was drafted, what was skipped and why, and what still needs a decision. Nothing happens quietly in the background.

It’s AI-assisted, not completely hands-off

Unusual invoices, ambiguous transactions and anything the agent isn’t confident about still require human input. That’s intentional.

It can usually be ready before your new hire starts

Once we have access to your Xero file and the inbox your invoices arrive in, the first working version can typically be ready in about a week. That should leave plenty of time between now and your new hire’s start date.
