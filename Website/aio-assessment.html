Questions You Might Have

How is this different from the free assessment?

The free assessment is a 15-minute chat that gives you one recommendation. This one looks across your whole business, with a 45-minute call and a report with three to seven recommendations.

How much of my time will it take?

Less than 2 hours in total, including the call and the walkthrough. You don’t need to prepare anything.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. You tell us how your business runs and we do the rest. The report is written in plain English, and we go through it with you.

We’ve already tried AI. Is this still worth it?

Yes. We look at the whole business, so you get a second opinion on what you’re already using and a clear view of what you haven’t tried.

Does the $1,200 include software or setup?

No. The fee covers the call, the report and the walkthrough. Each recommendation shows what it costs and how long it takes to set up. You can set them up yourself, or ask us to help.

Can we meet in person?

Yes, if you’re between Port Macquarie and SE QLD. We love sitting down face-to-face, because the best ideas often come from seeing how the work actually gets done. If you’re further away, we’ll do it online.

Is the call recorded?

Yes, so your report can be built from what you tell us. Our privacy policy explains how that’s handled.

How does the guarantee work?
