Who We Are

This policy covers Dark Horse AI (ABN 49 672 814 557), based in Coffs Harbour, NSW. It explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

How We Use Information

We may also share information where the law requires it.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and analytics tools to show us how visitors use the site, such as which pages they view. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Keeping Information Secure

We keep information for as long as we need it to provide our services, maintain business records and meet legal obligations, then delete it or remove anything that identifies you.

We take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, but no transmission over the internet is completely secure.
