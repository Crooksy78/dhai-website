Who We Are

This policy covers Dark Horse AI (ABN 49 672 814 557), based in Coffs Harbour, NSW. It explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

What We Collect

When you fill in our free assessment form, we collect:

  • Your name, business name, email address and, if you give it, your phone number
  • Your answers about your business, including the work you do, how long it takes and who does it
  • The hourly value of that time, if you choose to tell us

When you book a time, Cal.com collects your booking details. If you email us, we receive whatever you include in your message.

Our calls are recorded so your report can be built from what you tell us.

Please don’t send us passwords, customer lists or other sensitive information through the form.

How We Use It

  • To prepare for and run your assessment
  • To write your report and go through it with you
  • To contact you about your assessment, and about our services if they may help your business
  • To understand how people use our website and improve it

We don’t sell your personal information.

Who We Share It With

We use these service providers to run our business, and they handle information on our behalf:

  • Make, which moves form answers between our systems
  • Google, which stores form answers in Google Sheets and provides Google Analytics
  • ClickSend, which sends us a text alert with your name, business name and hours when you submit the form
  • SMTP2GO, which delivers an email of your answers to us
  • Cal.com, which handles bookings

Some of these providers store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States.

We may also use AI tools to help prepare your report. We may share your information if the law requires it.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics, which uses cookies to show us how visitors use the site, such as which pages they view. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Keeping Your Information

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, then delete it or remove anything that identifies you. We take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

Your Choices

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. You can also ask us to stop contacting you at any time. Just email us at craig​@​darkhorseai​.​com​.​au.

Questions or Complaints

If you have a question or complaint about your privacy, email us at the address above and we’ll respond as soon as we can. If you’re not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
